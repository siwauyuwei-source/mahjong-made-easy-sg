Singapore Mahjong essentials

How a turn works

Draw a tile (or claim a discard when allowed), then discard a tile. Players typically hold 13 tiles between turns and 14 when declaring a win.

Sets

Chow: three consecutive numbers of the same suit. Pong: three identical tiles. Kong: four identical tiles (with special replacement-draw rules). Pair: two identical tiles.

Local features

Singapore Mahjong commonly uses flowers and animal bonus tiles, and winning hands are valued in tai (fan). Exact minimum tai, payout and special-hand rules differ between tables, so agree on house rules before playing.

Important note

This is a beginner learning prototype. It does not calculate tai, resolve claims or replace a complete local ruleset.
